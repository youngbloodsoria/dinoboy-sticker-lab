How Orders Work

Merchant

The Roar Back Project is the merchant of record. We collect payment, applicable sales tax, and customer order details.

Bulk Orders

Confirmed paid orders are reviewed and grouped into bulk purchase orders for the manufacturing partner.

Production

The manufacturing partner creates the custom shoes, packs each order, and ships directly to the purchaser.

Impact

For every completed pair purchased, $25 helps fund a child’s custom sticker pack.

The Roar Back Project does not manufacture, paint, pack, or physically ship the shoes.
