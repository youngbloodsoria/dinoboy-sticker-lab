Our Process

🛒
1

You Order

A supporter selects a size and quantity and completes checkout on our website.

▤
2

We Collect

The Roar Back Project processes payment and applicable sales tax through Stripe.

☑
3

We Bulk Order

We review confirmed paid orders and combine them into a bulk order.

👟
4

They Create

Sneakcares, our partner in Indonesia, hand-paints, prepares, and packs each pair.

✈
5

They Ship

The manufacturing partner ships directly to each customer.

▱
6

You Get It

The customer receives the shoes and another child gets help creating their own roar.

$25 from every pair purchased = a kid gets to roar
